# Nyra attire and source-material finish — 054

The **original** Quaternius native65 adult head, anatomy, eight meshes, rest skeleton, UVs and skin remain in `../nyra052/arcanist.glb`, unchanged. The accepted physical staff/finger profile remains revision 053. `nyra-attire054.glb` is a separate, small accessory layer: a front-open midnight coat, split knee-length tails, bronze bindings, dark fitted lapel facings, fitted sleeve shoulder metal, and a small head-bound circlet. Its original 65 rest joints and inverse binds are copied exactly for named binding to the running source skeleton. The source skeleton is instantiated only once in the game; the accessory's import skeleton is discarded after extracting its meshes.

`source_avatar_style.gd` keeps the artist PBR maps on the source meshes and gives sleeves, corset, trousers, leather boots, silver hair, skin and eyes separate material values. It does not alter face geometry, replace normal maps, multiply the whole character blue or introduce facial blendshapes. Source materials remain independent per rendered mesh and actor. The fitted upper coat retains the original fine garment normal/ORM maps at their correctly interpolated source UVs, without the original brown diffuse color. Newly modeled tails use real mesh normals, metal/roughness response and physically modeled fine folds; they do not reuse an unrelated texture atlas. No generated or edited image is included.

The upper coat and shoulder details are fitted shells of indexed source garment surfaces. Continuous panel boundaries analytically clip source triangle edges, interpolating their original UVs, normals and native bone weights at new edge vertices. Existing source vertices keep their original native influences; up to four influences are retained for new cut vertices. The original eight meshes remain untouched. The new coat ends below the source standing collar instead of duplicating its folded rings. Each split tail inherits the nearest **same-side** source garment vertex's original influences. The tails have an open front and a split back, without a pelvis-rigid sheet bridging the walking legs. Their first ring matches the source waist and 78 mm half-opening, then smoothly flares to the knee hem; it does not begin as a disconnected wide rear shell. This is skinned clothing, not cloth simulation. Runtime garment probes measure all actual indexed weighted accessory vertices for grounded death; source foot contact and right-hand staff solving remain authoritative.

## Rebuild and provenance

```sh
python3 tools/art/build_nyra054_attire.py \
  --source assets/models/nyra052/arcanist.glb \
  --output assets/models/nyra054/nyra-attire054.glb
```

The deterministic JSON report records body/accessory hashes, exact native rig contract, mesh counts, bounds and weights. Source GLB weight sums differ from one by at most `1.2e-7`. Godot stores the actual renderer weights as UNORM16; the initial actual integration diagnostic measured a sum of `0.9999541901` (three quantization steps below one). The style smoke checks the derived four-influence precision budget `4/65535` plus float32 readback precision, as well as every actual animated indexed surface, floor contact, native inverse bind and independent material. Adapted shell geometry comes from **Quaternius**, acquired under **CC0 1.0** from the author's free Standard packages. The exact original license is also copied here as `QUATERNIUS-CC0-LICENSE.txt`. Exact supplied licenses and original source provenance remain in [nyra052](../nyra052/README.md); they also ship with the game. New Emberfall tail, binding and circlet geometry in this directory is provided under CC0 1.0.

This revision improves coherent character styling. It does not claim a new high-poly sculpt, baked cloth textures, simulated coat physics, individual strands, production facial animation or Diablo Immortal fidelity. Continuous gameplay and native Android validation are required to judge its delivered appearance.
