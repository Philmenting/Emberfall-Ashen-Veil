# Nyra — concrete character production brief

The requested result is an adult, convincingly animated dark-fantasy heroine inspired by the material and character craftsmanship of Diablo Immortal. The previous automatic helper-shell outfit and cap/tube hair remain rejected. The new generated reference establishes the intended appearance; it is a two-dimensional modeling reference, not a game asset or proof that equivalent three-dimensional art has been produced.

[Nyra modeling reference](reference-051/nyra-modeling-reference.png).

## Defined appearance

Nyra is approximately 32, with an athletic, credible adult outline, a distinctive composed face, grey eyes and swept silver hair gathered into a practical braid. Her complete face and both hands must remain recognizable in close view. The full figure must read clearly from Emberfall's ordinary isometric camera.

The outfit has a tailored midnight-blue split coat, separate facing and lapels, an ivory standing collar, a fitted worn brown leather cuirass, practical trousers and tall constructed boots. Restrained aged bronze provides a small asymmetric shoulder plate, fasteners and limited engraved decoration. There are two functional belt pouches. The staff is slender and coherently assembled, with a crescent head and a restrained turquoise crystal. Cloth, leather, skin, hair and metal need distinct believable surfaces.

The reference has significantly more sculptural and surface detail than the first code-built prototype. Those details must be modeled and textured deliberately. A generic anatomical head with a legacy diffuse map, strips that read as a helmet, rectangular cloth panels, exposed body through clothes, and featureless shiny boots do not satisfy the brief.

## Required source and integration

Deliver an editable Blender source and a real game-ready skinned GLB/FBX, with an unobstructed complete head, both hands and articulated fingers. Keep the existing verified native53 hierarchy when building on the current anatomical source. A different licensed authored rig requires a truthful new semantic map and retargeting; its compatibility is to be established from the actual source rather than promised from a preview.

Garments need independent sewn construction, joint clearance and appropriate skin transfer. Hair needs deliberately shaped volume, convincing strands/clumps and correct transparency where used. Source meshes, UVs, materials and skin influences must survive the actual export. Do not hide body intersections by deleting or shrinking anatomical parts.

Deliver actual authored albedo, tangent normal, roughness, metallic and ambient-occlusion maps with documented color spaces. Skin/face and hair require their own appropriate treatment. If high-poly detail is described as baked, supply the real high-poly source, cage, bake outputs and tested imported maps. A generated beauty image is not a normal-map bake or mesh source.

The first mobile target is approximately 50,000 total triangles, a small material set and 2K principal texture sets; this is an initial engineering budget requiring profiling, not a performance promise. Avoid increasing complexity merely to improve a studio close-up.

## Visible acceptance

Inspect the actual imported model in a neutral full-body/front/three-quarter view, a face/hand close-up, and the ordinary game camera with HUD and effects. Check idle, walk, strongest basic/skill/heavy windup and release, recovery and settled death. Clothing must cover its intended regions and retain a tailored outline through shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle motion. Staff contact, hand shape and body support must look credible.

Technical rest/skin/bounds/sole tests support that review. They do not establish aesthetic quality, smooth movement or release readiness. Continuous movement needs actual playback or interactive inspection. Mobile frame time, heat and touch responsiveness need real-device testing.
