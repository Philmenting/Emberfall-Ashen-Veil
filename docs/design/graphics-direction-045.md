# Graphics direction · 0.45

User request: a complete graphics overhaul, explicitly oriented toward **Diablo Immortal** (3 October 2026). This authorizes replacement of the previous bright painterly world. Original Emberfall characters, names, combat rules and content remain the product. The reference governs mature material realism, isometric spatial clarity and disciplined lighting, not copied assets.

## Direction contract

Latest steering: “Findest du nicht dass man mal die Gesichter überarbeiten müsste?”
The facial overhaul is part of this scope: Nyra must read as a coherent adult
heroine in all three class portraits, camp and combat. Inspect actual lit 3D
head geometry and skin, not only the source facial texture.

**THESIS:** A dangerous, inhabited ruin seen through a steady isometric combat camera. The fight owns the image; distant architecture supports its depth.

**OWN-WORLD:** Soot-black iron, worn silver edges, oxblood cloth, wet slate and aged ivory. Ember, jade and spectral mauve appear at physical light sources and actual spells. Broad quiet ground surrounds sharp silhouettes. Armor is layered and worn rather than uniformly glossy bronze.

**STORY:** Choose Nyra's class, recognize her equipment, enter a region with its own material ecology, read the guardian's actual danger zones, and return to a sheltered camp.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A steeper three-quarter camera puts the lit combat floor across the middle of the screen. Equipped Nyra and enemies stand against quieter masonry. Rear arches and buttresses give real parallax; the dark distant plate has no foreground actor or competing bright window. The existing readable HUD frames the action and preserves safe touch targets.

**FORM:** User-selected dark fantasy ARPG canon, specifically Diablo Immortal; native code-led realization with original generated environment plates and authored 3D geometry. The concept-seed launcher was attempted and returned 127 (absent); no seed or separate approved screenshot is invented. The user's named reference and delegated overhaul determine the direction.

**SIGNATURE AND MOTION:** Lit forged edges reveal the moving skeleton; grounded contact shadows and localized weapon energy mark contact. Camera anchoring and simulation-driven warning geometry remain authoritative. Reduced Motion and Battery retain usable combat cues.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
