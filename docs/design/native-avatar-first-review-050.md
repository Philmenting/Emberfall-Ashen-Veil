# First whole-avatar visual preflight — Nyra native 050

**Disposition: FAIL / bounded visual correction required.** This first complete adult whole-avatar construction is not yet a high-quality finished character. Keep the current native adult body and rest structure for the correction. The seven supplied frames do not justify replacing that body, changing the base, or reopening the previous microfix series.

This is an ordinary pixels-first source preflight, not native-game approval. ## Bounded correction list

1. **Hair blocks the face and intersects the outfit.** In every front frame, several thick opaque silver strips cover both eyes and almost all cheeks. Only a narrow central nose/lip opening remains. This prevents assessment of adult facial expression and identity. In `rest_front`, white wedges around the center chest and four white tabs below the bust are visible within the blue garment. In `rest_rear`, the silver strips disappear behind the upper blue back and reappear as a broad lower-back row above the belt. The interrupted silhouette reads as hair passing through clothing. Correct the existing hair silhouette, front opening, and clearance: expose the eyes and cheeks and keep the visible locks consistently outside the garment. Preserve the head/body proportions.

2. **The staff assembly and hand contact break in the supplied poses.** In `shoulder_reach` and `elbow_flex`, the cyan jewel and part of its silver surround stay at the top of the frame while the shaft and its upper metal end move far below or left of it. The visible gap is an assembly failure. In `wrist_finger_grip`, the staff-side hand rotates toward the shaft, but the fingers remain largely extended across/beside it instead of forming a closed hold. Correct the whole staff's coherent movement and the existing hand's wrap/contact together. The resting open hand can remain open; the named grip proof must visibly grip.

3. **Boot coverage fails in the leg/stance evidence.** In `hip_knee_flex`, flesh-toned toe-like lobes are visible below the raised boot's front edge. In `two_foot_stance`, similar lobes are visible below both boot fronts, while the front soles/collars form uneven curled rims. Close and fit the existing boot fronts and soles around the native feet across these exact poses. This should be a boot/coverage correction, not a change to foot proportions or rest bones.

4. **The main outfit remains a coarse blockout in silhouette and surface finish.** The chest is a smooth inflated blue shell; sleeves blend into rounded shoulders with little construction definition. The hip wrap has a bulky central lip and very thick, uneven side-panel silhouettes extending to the knees. The rear waist/panel transitions show little clear separation between fitted garment, belt, and hanging panels. Boots also read as soft smooth tubes. These large forms dominate every whole-body image and do not yet convey the requested mature dark-fantasy finish. Refine this existing outfit's edge thickness, fitted shoulder/hip transitions, deliberate panel shapes, and material definition in one bounded garment pass. Avoid adding accessory categories or replacing the native body.

## What the images support, and what they do not

The visible adult body silhouette is broadly coherent: head-to-body scale, arm length, pelvis-to-leg relation, and resting limb outlines do not show an obvious gross collapse. The exposed hands retain recognizable palms, thumbs, and separated finger silhouettes. I do not see a basis in these images for calling the entire native body deformed or for choosing a different base.

The face cannot be approved while its eyes and cheeks are concealed. The rendered outfit obscures the underlying shoulder and hip anatomy, so outfit silhouette defects should not be attributed to the body without further evidence.

The named pose images provide limited bend evidence. `shoulder_reach` mainly lowers the arms, `elbow_flex` retains a near-rest open-arm outline, and `hip_knee_flex` mainly shows a small foot elevation in this front view. They show no obvious catastrophic limb tear in the visible outlines, but they do not establish clean substantial elbow/knee flexion. This is a limit on what the supplied pixels demonstrate, not a request for new angles or a claim about unseen bone axes/weights.

The four items above are the complete first correction set for this whole-avatar phase. Recheck these same seven proof views after the correction. Do not infer game readiness from the source preflight.
